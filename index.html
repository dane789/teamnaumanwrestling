01AGES 4–10

ROOTS

Games, tumbling, agility, body-weight strength, basic rules, and introductory wrestling technique. Up to 20 wrestlers; one or two one-hour sessions per week.

02AGES 6–12

Saplings

Build on the basics through gymnastics, body control, and wrestling technique. Up to 20 wrestlers; one one-hour session per week, with possible extra practices. ROOTS graduates receive selection priority.
