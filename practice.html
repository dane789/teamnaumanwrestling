How partner groups work

Families see broad age and weight ranges and skill level when at least three wrestlers are in a group. Names and contact details stay in the private coach Sheet. Smaller groups are included in attendance totals without showing their details.

These groups help you plan; they are not assigned matchups. Coaches consider age, size, experience and how wrestlers work together before choosing partners. Planned attendance is not a guarantee someone will attend.
