FOR CLUB FAMILIES

Privacy & registration

Draft for club review. Registration services are not connected in this preview. The club must confirm its contact, permissions, retention period, and actual data practices before launch.

Using this preview

This draft has no analytics, advertising tools, or registration database. Practice filters do not save information about you. External links may open services operated by Google or the existing club website.

Registration requests

The proposed registration process uses Google Forms. Families would provide the minimum information needed for enrollment or a tournament request: parent or guardian contact details, wrestler name, age or division, program or event preference, and relevant weight class or availability.

A submitted request does not confirm enrollment, payment, tournament eligibility, or a roster place. Coaches must review and confirm those separately. Medical details, identity documents, and payment card details should not be entered in these general request forms.

Private club records

The proposed workflow stores form responses and tournament assignments in restricted Google Sheets and Drive folders accessible to designated club staff. Athlete names, family contact details, and team rosters will not be published in this website’s source files or public calendar.

Public schedules & documents

The public Google Calendar is for practice sessions, cancellations, clinics, and events. Public family resources should include only documents approved for sharing. Opening a calendar or resource loads a Google service; its privacy practices apply.

Photos & testimonials

Publish athlete photos and attributed testimonials only after the club confirms permission. Families must be able to contact the club to request review or removal.

Before this policy is finalized

  • Identify the club’s privacy contact and approved staff access.
  • Agree how long registration requests and roster records are retained and how deletion requests are handled.
  • Confirm parent or guardian consent, waiver collection, and photo permissions.
  • Update this page to reflect the services and practices actually in use.
