Website privacy & registration

Using the website

This website has no analytics, advertising tools or registration database. Its practice filters do not save information about you. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub’s privacy statement describes its handling of service data.

External club forms

Program and event links open existing club Google Forms. Information entered there goes to the form’s owner through Google, rather than to this website. Review each form’s details and requirements before submitting. Google’s privacy policy applies to its services.

Submitting a form does not by itself confirm payment, tournament eligibility or a roster place. A coach confirms your participation. Ask the club about availability, payment deadlines, refunds and its handling of registration records.

Public resources

The newsletter, event forms and other external resources may contain historical information. Confirm current dates, prices and your assigned session with the club. Directions open Google Maps; email links open your email application.

Questions about your information

For questions about club registration records, consent, photo permissions or removal requests, contact teamnaumanwrestlingclub@gmail.com.
