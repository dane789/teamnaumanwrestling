01YOUTH PROGRAM

Roots

A playful first step onto the mat. Give that big energy somewhere to go. Kids learn through tumbling, agility, body-weight exercises and wrestling games such as shoelace tag, flag tag and sumo. Along the way, they explore the rules and try foundational moves like a stance, shot, sprawl, half-nelson and stand-up. It’s an active introduction designed to make learning wrestling fun.

02YOUTH PROGRAM

Saplings

Keep the fun. Build the skills. For kids ready to grow beyond their first introduction, Saplings builds on wrestling fundamentals through gymnastics, body control and more technique. The emphasis stays on an enjoyable, active workout while children practice moving with purpose and putting their skills together.

Getting ready for youth wrestling

Beginners are welcome. Wear shorts and a T-shirt; avoid jeans, zippers and jewelry. Wrestling shoes are recommended, but beginners can ask the coach about starting without them. Parents may watch from the designated area. Confirm current requirements before your first visit.

More time to train. More value for your family.

Choose a plan that fits how you wrestle. Drop in when you can, build a regular routine with a monthly plan, or lower your average monthly cost by committing to six months or a year. Consistent practice gives wrestlers more chances to repeat technique, learn from coaches and grow alongside teammates.
