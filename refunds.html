FOR CLUB FAMILIES

Cancellations & refunds

To cancel, email teamnaumanwrestlingclub@gmail.com with the wrestler’s name, event or program, and payment details. Notice is measured when the email is received, in Eastern Time.

Camps & clinics

  • Seven or more days before the start: full refund.
  • Less than seven days, but at least 48 hours before the start: full club credit.
  • Less than 48 hours, after the start, or a no-show: no automatic refund or credit. Injury, illness and family emergencies may be reviewed.

Tournament team spots

A coach offers your spot by email; verified payment secures it. Cancel by the cancellation deadline stated in your offer for a full refund. After that deadline, refunds depend on an approved, paid replacement or costs the club can recover. If no cancellation deadline was supplied before payment, contact the club for an individual review.

Memberships

An unused plan canceled before it begins is refundable. Once it starts, missed practices do not qualify for an automatic refund. Injury or extended illness may qualify for an approved pause or credit.

Club cancellations

If TNWC cancels a hosted event before it starts, or cannot provide your paid tournament spot through its own decision, you may choose a full refund or credit. If a hosted event is rescheduled and you cannot attend, you may request a refund. External tournament cancellations depend on the organizer’s refund terms and recoverable fees. Travel, accommodation and fees paid directly to others are excluded.

Credits & processing

Club credits last 12 months, may be used within the same household, and remain subject to availability. Approved refunds are normally processed within ten business days, to the original payer. Existing registrations retain the terms supplied when you registered.
